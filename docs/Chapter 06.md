Chapter 06 Trust & Governance
===

● Point 1: Transparent Rules
Agent policies are clearly defined.
No hidden decisions are made.
Trust is established.
The system gains acceptance.

● Point 2: Independent Audits
Independent audits review the system.
Weaknesses are identified.
Opportunities for improvement appear.
Quality is maintained.

● Point 3: Community Governance
Community input is included.
Governance is shared.
Abuse is prevented.
Trust grows stronger.
