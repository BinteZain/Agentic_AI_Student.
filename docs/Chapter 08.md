Chapter 08 Recovery & Resilience
===

● Point 1: Identity Recovery
Identity recovery is essential if access is lost.
Users are not left alone.
Clear recovery procedures exist.
Trust is restored.

● Point 2: Social Recovery
Social recovery provides support.
Trusted contacts participate.
Abuse is minimized.
Recovery remains secure.

● Point 3: Emergency Controls
Emergency freeze options are available.
Damage is stopped immediately.
Time is gained for investigation.
System stability is preserved.
