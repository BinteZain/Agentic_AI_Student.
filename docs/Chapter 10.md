Chapter 10 Future Roadmap
===

● Point 1: Self-Learning Agents
Agents will continuously learn and adapt.
Decision quality will improve.
Human intervention will decrease.
Efficiency will increase.

● Point 2: Global Identity
Cross-border identity acceptance will grow.
Geographical barriers will reduce.
Digital services will expand.
Global trust will strengthen.

● Point 3: User-Centric Identity
Identity will be fully user-controlled.
Data will become a personal asset.
AI agents will negotiate trust autonomously.
A self-sovereign digital future will emerge.
