Chapter 01  Vision & Problem
===

● Point 1: Identity Crisis
Digital identity fraud is increasing rapidly.
Current systems cannot keep up with this growth.
Users feel insecure about their identities.
A new model is urgently needed.

● Point 2: Weak Centralized Systems
Centralized identity systems suffer from data leaks.
A single failure affects the entire system.
User trust is continuously declining.
Decentralized approaches offer better security.

● Point 3: User Ownership
Users have limited control over their data.
Information is often used without consent.
Lack of transparency breaks trust.
Agentic models return control to users.
