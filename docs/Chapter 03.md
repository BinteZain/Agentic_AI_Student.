Chapter 03 Identity Signals
===

● Point 1: Government Credentials
Official documents serve as primary proof.
Alone, they are not sufficient.
Additional signals are required.
Overall trust is strengthened.

● Point 2: Biometric Evidence
Biometrics provide unique identification.
They are difficult to fake.
Accuracy increases significantly.
Security risks are reduced.

● Point 3: Behavioral Analysis
Behavioral data builds over time.
Usage patterns are continuously observed.
Suspicious activity is detected early.
Continuous protection becomes possible.
